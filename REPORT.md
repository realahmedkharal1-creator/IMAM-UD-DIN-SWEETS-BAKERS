## 4. Brand & Design Rationale

- **Color Palette**:
  - *Warm Gold & Ochre (`#B45309`, `#F59E0B`)*: Symbolizes pure desi ghee, saffron spices, and traditional royal Pakistani halwai heritage.
  - *Deep Royal Maroon (`#92400E`)*: Evokes festive celebratory warmth, wedding banquets, and timeless artisanal craft.
  - *Warm Cream Canvas (`#FAF8F5`)*: Replaces harsh clinical white with appetizing, organic warm tones.
  - *Emerald Green (`#047857`)*: High-trust conversion color for WhatsApp ordering and free delivery badges.

- **Typography System**:
  - *Cinzel (Display)*: Majestic classical serif giving a premium, trustworthy look to logos and headings.
  - *Playfair Display (Subheadings)*: Elegant editorial serif that elevates dessert and sweet descriptions.
  - *Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & UI)*: Clean, ultra-legible modern sans-serif optimized for mobile speed and microcopy.

- **Layout & Interaction Architecture**:
  - *Custom Cake Builder*: Turns passive menu browsing into active engagement by calculating weight, tiers, and flavors in real time with instant WhatsApp forwarding.
  - *Real-Time Open/Closed Status*: Calculates store status dynamically using Pakistan Time (`Asia/Karachi`), preventing customer frustration after closing hours.
